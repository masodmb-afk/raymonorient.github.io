About Us

Raymon Orient Trading FZE is a UAE-based trading company specialized in metals and non-ferrous materials, providing transparent and well-documented trade operations for industrial clients. We focus on reliability, compliance, and long-term partnerships across the GCC and Asia.

Our Focus

  • Base metals — Zinc (SHG / RHG / HG), Aluminum, Copper, Lead
  • Bundle-wise identification & verified zinc documentation (ASTM B6 aligned)
  • Reliable coordination with UAE manufacturers & galvanizing plants

Our Services

  • Structured commercial offers based on international Incoterms (CIF Jebel Ali, EXW warehouse delivery)
  • Full documentation package (Invoice, Packing List, COA, BL, Certificate of Origin)
  • Bundle-wise identification with unique traceable codes (Bundle No, Lot No, Grade, Weight, Year, Full Code)
  • Coordination with logistics, surveyors, or inspection companies when required
  • End-to-end communication from quotation to delivery
